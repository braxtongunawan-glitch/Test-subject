# Privacy Policy

> DRAFT - must be reviewed and approved by Indonesian counsel before production use. The Bahasa Indonesia version prevails.

1. **Data Controller.** The Controller's identity and contact details are on the Contact page.
2. **Data processed.** User account data (name, e-mail, authentication factors), contact data of patent owners and their representatives (name, e-mail, phone, role), public patent data from PDKI, consent records and audit logs.
3. **Purposes and legal bases (Law No. 27/2022 Art. 20).** Performance of contracts with clients; legal obligations; legitimate interest for relevant notices about specific patents, supported by a documented Legitimate Interest Assessment; and consent for service offers.
4. **Retention.** Data is kept as long as needed for these purposes; consent and audit records as required by law. Opted-out e-mail addresses are kept as a keyed hash so they are never contacted again.
5. **Data subject rights.** Information, access, rectification, erasure, withdrawal of consent and objection to processing. Use the Controller's contact details or the preferences link in every e-mail.
6. **Security.** Encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access control, multi-factor authentication and tamper-evident audit logs.
7. **International transfers.** Where they occur, in accordance with Art. 56 of the PDP Law.
8. **Personal data breaches.** Notification to data subjects and the relevant authority within 3 x 24 hours as required by the PDP Law.
