> DRAFT - must be reviewed by Indonesian counsel. The Bahasa Indonesia version prevails.

By uploading or managing data about patent owners, inventors and third-party contacts you confirm that: (a) your organisation has a lawful basis to process that data; (b) it is used only for patent monitoring and relevant correspondence; (c) the source and legal basis of prospect contacts are documented; and (d) data subject requests will be forwarded and handled on time.
